# Pending approval and account invitations

Signed in to NetBird and landed on a **Waiting for Approval** screen, or in a NetBird account you don't recognize? This usually is not a bug. It almost always means you were added to an existing organization automatically, and someone in that organization still has to approve you.

## What the Waiting for Approval screen tells you

After you sign in, the dashboard shows three steps: **Account Created**, **Waiting for Approval**, and **Join Account**. Your user exists, but the account requires new users to be approved before they can use it, so you are held at the second step until someone approves you.

Below the steps, the screen names the owner of the account you are waiting to join, for example `li****g@example.com`. Most of the part before the `@` is hidden to protect the owner's privacy, but the domain is shown in full. That domain is the quickest way to tell which organization you are waiting on.

If the screen only says "Ask the owner of the account to approve your access", with no address, NetBird could not look up the owner's email. Everything below still applies.

The screen has two buttons:

- **Refresh** checks again whether you have been approved. Use it once someone tells you they approved you.
- **Log Out** signs you out. Use it if you signed in with the wrong account, or you don't want to join this one.

## Why this happens

NetBird can add people to an account without a manual, one-by-one invite:

- **Indirect (domain) invites.** If an account has verified your email domain, new sign-ups with that domain join it automatically. See [Indirect user invites](/manage/team/add-users-to-your-network#indirect-user-invites).
- **Identity provider sync.** If your organization connects NetBird to its identity provider, being added to a synced group in the IdP provisions your user in NetBird. See [Provision users and groups from your identity provider](/manage/team/idp-sync).
- **Approval is required.** When an account turns on [user approval](/manage/team/approve-users#require-user-approval), new users stay blocked until an admin approves them. That block is the Waiting for Approval screen you see.

## What to do

1. **Check the domain on the screen.** It is normally your organization's email domain. Confirm with your IT or security team that they run NetBird and expect you on it.
2. **Ask the owner or an admin to approve you.** The screen names the account [Owner](/manage/team/user-roles#owner), of which there is exactly one per account, but any admin can approve you as well. They do it from the dashboard's user list. See [Approve or reject user](/manage/team/approve-users#approve-or-reject-user).
3. **Click Refresh once you are approved.** The screen moves you into the account. If it still shows Waiting for Approval, your browser may be holding on to a cached session: open the dashboard in a private browser window and sign in again.
4. **If something looks off,** for example the domain is not your organization's, you don't recognize the account, or no one internally owns it, do not ask anyone to approve you. Click **Log Out**, treat it as suspicious, and verify internally first.

<Note>
On self-hosted NetBird, the Waiting for Approval screen needs dashboard v2.93.0 or later, and the owner's address on it needs management v0.80.0 or later. Older dashboards show a **User Approval Pending** error page with only a **Sign Out** button instead: the steps above still apply, but after you are approved, sign out and sign in again.
</Note>

<Note>
Still unsure who owns the account, or think you were added to the wrong one? Reach out through [NetBird Support](/help/netbird-support) and the team can help confirm the account and its owner.
</Note>
