# Troubleshooting

Something not working as expected? Check the common issues below, or browse by area.

## Common issues

The problems support sees most often.

<div className="xl:max-w-none [&_table]:table-fixed [&_th]:w-1/2 [&_th:last-child]:pl-8 [&_td:last-child]:pl-8">

| Symptom | Start here |
|---|---|
| The dashboard shows **Waiting for Approval** after you sign in | [Pending approval and account invitations](/help/troubleshooting-account-access) |
| The client keeps asking you to log in again, or a peer shows `Login required` | [Peer session expiration](/manage/settings/enforce-periodic-user-authentication) |
| A peer shows `Relayed` instead of `P2P` | [Troubleshooting relayed connections](/help/troubleshooting-relayed-connections) |
| The tunnel works but is slow, or takes long to connect | [Troubleshooting performance](/help/troubleshooting-performance) |
| A service behind a routing peer is unreachable, or a new resource never shows up | [Troubleshooting resource connectivity](/help/troubleshooting-resource-connectivity) |
| A network or host firewall blocks NetBird, or traffic stops at a security product such as a SASE or SSL-inspection service | [Ports & firewalls](/about-netbird/ports-and-firewalls) <br /> On the peer itself: [Symptoms of host-based firewall issues](/about-netbird/ports-and-firewalls#symptoms-of-host-based-firewall-issues) |
| Names do not resolve, or DNS stops working after NetBird disconnects | [DNS troubleshooting](/manage/dns/troubleshooting) |
| A NetBird Reverse Proxy service returns `502`, times out, or its certificate is never issued | [Reverse proxy troubleshooting](/manage/reverse-proxy/troubleshooting) <br /> Certificate: [Certificate is not issued after verification](/manage/reverse-proxy/custom-domains#certificate-is-not-issued-after-verification) |
